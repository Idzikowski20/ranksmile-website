Three paid plans. The differences are mostly about scale: how many brands you run, how much you publish, and how widely you track.

## What each plan includes

|                                | Growth       | Scale    | Agency    |
| ------------------------------ | ------------ | -------- | --------- |
| Brand Spaces                   | 5            | 15       | Unlimited |
| Documents                      | 30           | 100      | Unlimited |
| AI prompts                     | 50 daily     | 100      | 250 daily |
| AI visibility engines          | 4            | All 5    | All 5     |
| Keyword research per month     | 200          | 500      | 2,000     |
| Competitor keyword gap         | 25 per month | Included | Included  |
| Content ideas and coverage gap | Included     | Included | Included  |
| Advanced SERP analysis         |              | Included | Included  |
| Site audit pages per crawl     |              |          | 1,000     |
| Personalized onboarding        |              |          | Included  |

Prices and billing options are on the [pricing page](/pricing). Everything is billed monthly or yearly, VAT excluded, and you can cancel at any point.

## The trial

Seven days on Growth, once per organization. It is the full plan, not a restricted demo.

Scale and Agency start paid. Nothing is deleted when a trial ends; your data waits for you to pick a plan.

[Your first week](/docs/introduction/first-week) is written around the trial length, and the order in it matters: some of what Ranksmile measures only becomes readable with a few days of history behind it.

## Which limit you hit first

In practice, one of two.

**Brand Spaces**, if you are an agency. Client count grows faster than anything else, and five goes quickly.

**Documents**, if you are one company publishing steadily. Thirty is comfortable for a few months and then is not.

The others rarely bind. Two hundred keyword research queries a month is more than most teams use, and the daily prompt allowance is generous relative to how many questions are actually worth tracking. See [choosing prompts](/docs/ai-visibility/prompts) for why tracking fewer, better questions beats filling the allowance.

<Admonition type="note" title="Engines on Growth">
Growth tracks four of the five answer engines. Scale and Agency track all five. Which engines those are, and what the difference means for what you can see, is covered in [AI visibility](/docs/ai-visibility/overview).
</Admonition>

## Changing plan

Upgrades take effect immediately and the new limits apply at once. Downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period, so you keep what you paid for.

If you downgrade below your current usage, nothing is deleted. The spaces and documents over the new limit become read-only until you are back under it.
