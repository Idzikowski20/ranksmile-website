Ranksmile watches how findable you are, in both places that matter now: the classic search results, and the answers AI assistants give when someone asks about your market.

Those two used to be the same problem. They no longer are. A page can hold first position and still be absent from every generated answer above it, and a page nobody ranks can be the one an assistant quotes. Ranksmile measures both, shows you where they disagree, and turns the gaps into work you can actually do.

## How the pieces fit

Everything starts from a [Brand Space](/docs/introduction/brand-spaces): one domain, with its own keywords, crawls, articles and results. Inside a space the modules feed each other rather than sitting in separate tabs.

A [site audit](/docs/site/site-audit) finds the technical problems and checks whether answer-engine crawlers can reach you. [Keyword research](/docs/search/keyword-research) finds the terms worth having. [Keyword tracking](/docs/search/keyword-tracking) follows what happens to them. [AI visibility](/docs/ai-visibility/overview) watches the generated answers for the same topics. [Content score](/docs/content/content-score) tells you why a specific page is not competing, and [Automations](/docs/content/automations) schedules the work that fixes it.

[Recommendations](/docs/site/recommendations) is where they meet: one ordered list drawn from all of it, so you are not deciding which of four dashboards to believe.

<Admonition type="tip" title="If you only do one thing first">
Run a site audit and open the AI Search Health result. A blocked crawler explains a flat AI visibility line faster than any amount of content analysis, and it is the one problem you can fix in an afternoon.
</Admonition>

## Who it is for

Teams who own a site and need to know whether it is winning, and agencies running that job for several clients at once. Agencies get a [Brand Space per client](/docs/introduction/brand-spaces), with nothing shared between them.
