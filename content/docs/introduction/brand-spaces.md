A Brand Space is one domain and everything Ranksmile knows about it. You create one per site you are responsible for.

Nothing crosses between spaces. Keywords tracked in one do not show up in another, a crawl belongs to the space that ran it, and an article scored in one client's space is invisible in the next. That isolation is the point: it is what makes the numbers readable when you are running ten of them.

## What lives in a space

<DefinitionList>

Keywords
: The terms you track, the ones research surfaced, and the gaps a competitor owns

Crawls
: Every site audit run, with the history to compare against

Articles
: What you have written, scored and published, with the optimization history behind each one

AI visibility
: The prompts you track, the answers they returned, and which sources got cited

Activity
: The log of what ran, what changed, and who changed it

</DefinitionList>

## How many you need

One per domain. If you own a single site, one space is the whole product.

If you manage several brands under one company, resist the urge to put them in one space. Shared keyword lists across brands make the data harder to read, not easier: a position that moved is suddenly ambiguous about which brand moved, and content scores compare pages against the wrong competitive set.

How many you can have depends on your plan. See [plans and limits](/docs/introduction/plans).

## Setting one up

You need two things: the domain, and access to its Search Console property.

Search Console is optional in the sense that the product works without it, but [Performance](/docs/search/performance) stays empty until you connect it, and performance data is what gives the rest of the numbers context. Connect it on day one, because the history it pulls in is more useful the longer it has been there.

<Callout title="Working with clients">
For agency work, ask the client for Search Console access before you create the space. It is the step most likely to take a week of waiting, and everything else is faster once it is done.
</Callout>

## Switching between them

The space you are in is shown at the top of every module, and switching carries you to the same module in the new space rather than dropping you at a dashboard. When you are comparing the same report across three clients, that is the difference between three clicks and nine.
