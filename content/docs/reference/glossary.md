<DefinitionList>

AI Search Health
: Whether the crawlers behind the answer engines can reach your pages. Reported per crawler as Healthy, Have issues, or Blocked. See [AI Search Health](/docs/site/ai-search-health)

AI visibility
: Whether assistants name your brand and cite your domain when answering questions in your market. Distinct from rank tracking, which measures the classic results. See [AI visibility](/docs/ai-visibility/overview)

Answer engine
: A surface that generates an answer rather than returning a list of links. Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini

Auto-Optimize
: A run that closes the gaps a content score found and reports what changed. Nothing publishes without approval. See [Auto-Optimize](/docs/content/auto-optimize)

Brand Space
: One domain and everything Ranksmile knows about it. Nothing crosses between spaces. See [Brand Spaces](/docs/introduction/brand-spaces)

Citation
: An engine linking your domain as a source for its answer. Different from a mention

Content score
: How a page compares to the pages currently winning its target query, with the breakdown of what is missing. See [content score](/docs/content/content-score)

Core, supporting, long-tail
: The priority you assign a tracked prompt, which sets how often it re-scans: daily, weekly, or every two weeks

Coverage gap
: A topic your content does not answer at all, as opposed to answers it badly

Fanout query
: A sub-question an engine generates from your prompt before answering it. Where the citation decision actually happens. See [fanout queries](/docs/ai-visibility/fanout-queries)

Keyword gap
: Terms a competitor ranks for and you do not. Produced by [keyword research](/docs/search/keyword-research)

Mention
: An engine naming your brand in its answer, whether or not it links you. Different from a citation

Prompt
: A question you track in AI visibility, put to every engine you have enabled. See [choosing prompts](/docs/ai-visibility/prompts)

Site Health
: The technical state of the pages a crawl covered, as a share of checks that passed. See [site audit](/docs/site/site-audit)

Site Speed
: How fast the site is, measured during the crawl rather than separately, so it is comparable to the issues listed beside it

Source
: A page cited in an answer, yours or a competitor's. See [Sources](/docs/ai-visibility/sources)

</DefinitionList>
