Seven days on the **Growth** plan, once per organization. Scale and Agency don't have a trial; they start paid, and you can cancel from the billing page at any point.

The trial is the full Growth plan, not a demo mode: five Brand Spaces, fifty daily AI prompts, thirty documents, two hundred keyword research queries a month, and competitor keyword gap analysis.

## Making a week count

Seven days is enough to see the shape of your visibility, but only if you start the slow things on day one.

1. **Add your domain and run Site Audit first.** The crawl takes a while and everything else reads better once you know what's broken. Check AI Search Health in the result: a blocked answer-engine crawler explains a flat AI visibility line better than any content theory will.
2. **Connect Search Console.** Performance data needs history, and pulling it in early means the rest of the week has context.
3. **Set up ten AI prompts on day one, marked core.** Core re-scans daily, so by day seven you have a week of trend instead of a single sample. This is the step people leave until day four and then can't read the result.
4. **Track your real keywords, not your brand name.** You already rank for your brand.
5. **Run keyword research against one competitor.** The gap report is the fastest honest answer to what you're missing.

## What happens at the end

Nothing is deleted when the trial ends. Your data stays; you pick a plan to keep working with it. Prices and limits are on the [pricing page](/pricing), billed monthly or yearly, VAT excluded.

<Admonition type="note" title="Before you start the clock">
Have your Search Console access ready and know which competitor you want to compare against. Both take minutes to arrange and both gate a step above.
</Admonition>
