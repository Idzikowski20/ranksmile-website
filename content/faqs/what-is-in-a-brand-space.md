A Brand Space is one domain and everything Ranksmile knows about it. Keywords, crawls, articles, scores and AI visibility results all belong to it, and nothing leaks between spaces. Agencies get one per client; a single company usually needs one.

## What's inside

**Search performance**

- **Performance** pulls your Search Console data into the same place as everything else, so impressions sit next to the audit that explains them.
- **Keyword tracking** follows your positions, refreshed daily.
- **Keyword list** is the working set: what you track, what you found, what you haven't acted on.
- **Keyword Research** finds new terms from a seed, with competitor gap analysis alongside.

**The site itself**

- **Site Audit** crawls the domain and reports what's broken, plus an AI Search Health check on whether the answer-engine crawlers can actually reach your pages.
- **Recommendations** turns those findings into an ordered list of things to do.
- **Content Audit** scores what you've published against what currently ranks for it.

**AI visibility**

Five views: **Overview** for the trend, **Sources** for which of your pages get cited, **Competitors** for who gets named instead of you, **Prompts** for what's being asked, and **Fanout Queries** for the follow-up questions an engine generates from one prompt.

**Running it**

- **Automations** handles the content calendar: scheduling, publishing and the recurring work.
- **Activity Log** is the history. Who changed what, which scan ran when, what a job returned.

## How many you need

One per domain you're responsible for. Growth includes five, Scale fifteen, Agency is uncapped.

If you manage several brands under one company, separate spaces are still the right call. Shared keyword lists across brands make the data harder to read, not easier.
