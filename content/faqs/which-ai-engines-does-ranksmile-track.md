Five engines: **Google AI Overviews**, **Google AI Mode**, **ChatGPT**, **Perplexity** and **Gemini**.

Each prompt you track is sent to every engine you've enabled, and each answer is checked for two things: whether your brand is mentioned, and whether your domain is cited as a source. Those are separate results. An engine can describe your product accurately and still link to a competitor's comparison page, which is the case worth catching early.

## Why these five

AI Overviews and AI Mode are where Google puts generated answers above the classic results, so they change what a first-position ranking is actually worth. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini are the assistants people open instead of a search box.

Claude is not tracked. When it is, it will appear in the engine list without you needing to change anything.

## Where the answers come from

Ranksmile queries the engines through [DataForSEO](https://dataforseo.com), not through its own scrapers. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini come back through their LLM response endpoints; AI Overviews and AI Mode come from Google SERP endpoints, because that's where those answers actually live.

This matters for one reason: the results are what the engine returned to a neutral query from a neutral location, not what it returns to you while you're logged in and it already knows your site.

<Admonition type="note" title="Prompt limits">
Fifty prompts per Brand Space. Each one runs against every enabled engine, so the ceiling is 250 prompt/engine pairs per scan.
</Admonition>

## Picking prompts that tell you something

Track the questions a buyer asks before they know your name. "Best rank tracker for agencies" says more about your visibility than "Ranksmile", which will mention you regardless and teaches you nothing.

Start with your top ten buying-intent questions, let them run a week, then add the long tail once you can see which topics you're absent from.
