# Ranksmile Pricing Plans

> AI visibility tracking, a live Content Score in the editor, and daily rank tracking, across three plans: Growth, Scale, and Agency. Flat monthly price per workspace, no usage metering. Growth starts with a 7-day free trial.

## How billing works

Plans are flat subscriptions billed per **workspace**, monthly or yearly. There is no usage metering and no overage billing: when a monthly allowance runs out, that action pauses until the next period or until you upgrade.

| Unit                  | Meaning                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AI prompt             | One question tracked against the AI engines on your plan. Metered **per day**.                 |
| Document              | One page you create or optimize in the editor, with its own Content Score and history.         |
| Brand Space           | One brand, product, or client: its own site, prompts, competitors, keywords, and reports.      |
| Keyword research      | One seed keyword expanded into related queries with volume and difficulty. Metered per month.  |
| Competitor gap run    | One comparison of your site against one competitor domain. Metered per month.                  |

All prices are in EUR and exclude VAT. Yearly billing takes 17% off every plan. You can change plan or cancel at any time from billing settings: upgrades are immediate and prorated, downgrades take effect at the end of the current period.

## Table clarifications

- **AI engines** are Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. Growth tracks four of the five; Scale and Agency track all five.
- **Re-scan cadence** depends on the priority you give a prompt: core daily, supporting weekly, long-tail every two weeks. The scheduler checks for due scans every six hours, and manual refresh is available after a short cooldown on the same tiers.
- **Rank tracking** runs once a day per tracked keyword, desktop and mobile, per country. It is not affected by the AI prompt allowance.
- **Documents** count only pages opened in the editor. Auditing a published page does not consume one.
- **Keyword research** and **Competitor Keyword Gap** reset monthly and unused runs do not roll over.
- **Site Audit** limits are per crawl, not per month.
- **White-label** covers client-facing reporting on the Agency plan; API access on Scale and Agency covers reporting and automation.
- **Unlimited documents and Brand Spaces** on Agency are subject to fair use; if you need more than the plan comfortably carries, contact us for a quote.

## Common questions

### What do I get in the free trial?

Growth comes with a 7-day free trial of the full plan: add a site, pick prompts, run the first scan, write in the editor, and publish to WordPress. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged.

### How is AI visibility measured?

Your tracked prompts are sent to each engine on your plan. Ranksmile stores the answer text together with the web citations it returned, then works out which brands and URLs were named. Your Visibility Score is the share of tracked prompts where your brand is named, reported per engine, alongside the cited sources, the competing brands, and the fanout queries the engine expanded your prompt into.

### What counts against my daily AI prompt allowance?

Each tracked prompt counts once per scan, regardless of how many engines answer it. Prompts that are not due for a re-scan that day do not count.

### What happens when I hit a plan limit?

Nothing is deleted. The action that hit the limit pauses: keyword research and competitor gap runs resume next month, document and Brand Space limits block creating new ones until you free one up or upgrade. Upgrading lifts any limit immediately.

### How can I control what I spend?

| Action                                         | Effect                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Mark prompts supporting or long-tail           | Fewer re-scans, allowance lasts longer            |
| Keep only buyer-intent prompts tracked         | Smaller daily prompt count                        |
| Pay yearly                                     | 17% off every plan                                |
| Archive Brand Spaces you no longer report on   | Frees a slot without upgrading                    |
